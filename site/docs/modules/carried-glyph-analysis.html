Track the visible ink of carried glyphs

scripts/reference/glyphs.py --props adds stable prop tracks to the existing component and event report for frames at their original resolution. A template taken from a starting frame follows moving ink without assigning a new identity every time another stroke obscures part of a glyph. The tool tracks pixels; it does not perform optical character recognition (OCR) or interpret an equation.

python3 scripts/reference/glyphs.py original-film.mp4 --from 0 --to 5 \
  --props --prop-seeds seeds.json --states states.json --font original.ttf \
  --size 72 --x 40 --y 150 --camera camera.json --out glyph-tracks.json

The examples refer to your own footage and font. Reference films, crops and tracks derived from them stay local under the benchmark's existing policy. The checked-in validation uses only original synthetic ink and a first-party fixture font.

Give seeds.json up to 32 time windows. Each window chooses a starting box around the glyph to track.

[{"id":"carried-term","from":0,"to":5,"seed":[20,55,20,29]}]

Times are clip-local seconds after --ref-offset/--rec-offset; windows are half-open and must lie inside --from/--to. The seed is a screen-pixel [x,y,width,height] box at the first decoded native frame in that window. Use a clean, unoccluded initial glyph. IDs are unique strings of 1–128 characters. Seed dimensions are 2–512 px with at most 131,072 pixels. The tracker crops to actual seed ink and retains that template; it cannot reconstruct a portion already missing from the seed.

Without --prop-seeds, existing carried events supply candidates. Candidates need independently moving ink over at least three supported frames; stationary controls are rejected. A supplied camera converts the motion check to world coordinates. Otherwise, a median shift from at least three recurring component identities removes common line translation. This fallback does not solve an unknown zoom or rotation. Overlapping continuous candidates merge their source component IDs under the earlier prop ID.

--states and --font must be supplied together. States are increasing [[time,text],...] or [{"t":time,"text":text},...]. Expected line ink uses the library's actual imported-font advances, kerning and stroke-built symbols through line_layout.py, then a 2 px dilation excludes that ink before tracking. Set --size, --x, --y and --letter-spacing to the authored line's world layout. States change at their authored times. This is an expected-line mask, so inaccurate states/layout/camera may erase valid prop ink; unsupported frames remain gaps rather than being declared successful tracks.

Supply --camera as a camera-track list or an object with a samples array. Each row records time, zoom and pan as shown below.

[{"t":0,"zoom":1,"panX":0,"panY":0},
 {"t":5,"zoom":1,"panX":20,"panY":0}]

Standard analyzer textCamera/cameraPosition samples are also accepted. Screen coordinates equal zoom * world + pan. The shared CameraTrack interpolates only adjacent known samples, does not extrapolate, and preserves unknown pan as a gap. Positive uniform zoom and translation are supported; rotation, skew and reflection refuse. A window whose first frame has unknown camera cannot acquire a seed later using stale seed coordinates.

The explainerkit.glyph-events/1 report remains intact. Its additional propTracks field uses explainerkit.glyph-props/1 and contains tracks, rejected, fps and sourceOffset. Each track records one stable id, its time window, source component IDs, independent displacement and the frame measurements described below.

FieldMeaning
time, frameClip-local seconds and original source-frame index
status: "observed"Unclipped seed-template extent with at least 98% ink support
status: "estimated"Template extent supported by partial ink, including clipping/occlusion
status: "gap"Unsupported continuity; boxes are null
screenBoxFull template extent; estimated rows explicitly include inferred hidden extent
observedBoxBounds of visible matching ink only
worldBoxInverse camera transform of screenBox; null without a camera
support, reasonVisible-template support fraction and the evidence/refusal reason

--prop-search sets the local prediction search radius, an integer 2–128 px (default 32). After more than 12 unsupported frames the tracker stops reacquiring that prop, avoiding a later unrelated glyph taking its identity. A new explicit window can start a new evidence interval. Camera jumps, large rotations, changing glyph shape and long total occlusion can exceed this translation/scale tracker; it does not invent a successful observation there.

Calls are bounded to 20,000 decoded frames, 200,000 output rows and 16 million pixels per frame (each side at most 8192). Rescaled templates are at most 1024 px per side and 262,144 pixels. Excess work refuses rather than silently truncating the reported evidence.

Run node --test tests/reference/props.test.mjs. The synthetic acceptance counts all visible truth frames, including gaps and wrong positions, in its within-3-px denominator. It tests a moving glyph across actual line ink, fragmenting occlusion, off-screen exit, stationary controls, duplicate source fragments, camera gaps and full CLI/event compatibility. Passing this test is not a claim about a local reference film's likeness or extraction accuracy.
