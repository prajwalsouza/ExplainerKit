Biped proportions, motion and outlined heads

stick({style:'heavy'}) creates a character with a 52-unit resting stance, about 0.3 of its 174-unit height. To change the feet without replacing the other proportions, supply build:{foot:26}. The override is applied to that style’s own build.

Walk/run plans account for the resting stance and available leg reach, including horizontal extrema during each smooth foot swing. Wider stances receive more, shorter steps when needed to keep knees above the floor. Lift and bob scale with leg length. Feet remain planted in world coordinates during support; zero speed is finite. Plans refuse more than 10,000 steps with RESOURCE_LIMIT.

The shared shoulder calculation and exposed poseLayout metadata support keyed hand targets and root-follow IK. A rig's explicit reach constraint can lower its hip when a posed foot needs more vertical reach.

Classic, xkcd and heavy outlined heads have transparent interiors by default. Set paper explicitly when a painted face disc is intended. Solid and skin styles retain their paint, and small eye and lid details keep their light-stage treatment. Recorded character-build measurements compare SVG and Canvas output, including edge differences, without claiming pixel-identical results for every background.

auditCharacter(definition,{background:'#000000'}) reports contrast and a warnings array. An opaque contrasting head disc produces HEAD_FILL_CONTRAST when its luminance contrast ratio is at least 3 against both ink and background. This artistic diagnostic is separate from geometric pass/fail and does not certify text accessibility. Missing/translucent background is unverified; unsupported solid colors are rejected by the paint validator. Transparent parts cannot connect otherwise detached artwork in the audit. Explicit encloses:true outlines can connect same-bone details contained inside them, so face details remain connected without an invisible painted connector.

The native character suite audits every starter across every action and includes focused checks for wide stances, zero speed and head construction. Run node benchmarks/character-builds.mjs to compare original synthetic SVG and Canvas pixels. The report at benchmarks/results/character-builds.json records a fingerprint of the source used.
