Credits for recordings and footage

The live demonstrations in these guides play recorded video and audio made by other people. This page lists each source and the licence it is used under. The demos credit their footage beside the picture as well, so this page collects those credits in one place and adds the instrument recordings that the music guides are rendered from.

The video effects guide tracks surfaces, recovers cameras and places graphics in recorded shots. Recordings behind the music guides

The audio in the music and music production guides was rendered by ExplainerKit. Where a piece uses recorded instruments, the notes come from the following collections. ExplainerKit selected pitches and takes, trimmed long releases, applied a short fade, a low cut and, for the piano, a high cut, and balanced the levels of each velocity layer.

  • Salamander Grand Piano v3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under CC BY 3.0. The adapted files use two recorded velocities for each chosen pitch. Original recordings and licence.
  • Karoryfer × Bigcat Cello by Karoryfer Samples and Bigcat Instruments, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The adapted files use sustained bow strokes and pizzicato attacks. Original recordings and licence.
  • VSCO 2 Community Edition violin by Versilian Studios, under CC0 1.0. Source.

The modeled piano in explainerkit/sound is calibrated against measurements of the Salamander Grand Piano recordings. That calibration data ships in the npm package and carries the same CC BY 3.0 attribution.
