ExplainerKit is a JavaScript library for explanations that move, respond and rewind.

explainerkit/geometry

Paths that draw themselves.

Build, measure and deform curves and shapes. Reveal any path, carry anything along it, and keep an even pace by arc length.

explainerkit/physicsexplainerkit/noise

Motion that responds.

Springs, oscillators and live pointer velocity, with seeded fields and repeatable randomness. Every frame can be replayed exactly.

explainerkit/proceduralexplainerkit/import/svg

Generated, not placed.

Scatter by Poisson sampling, along paths or through fields, so nothing looks arranged. Bring your own SVG and it becomes geometry.

explainerkit/layoutexplainerkit/spatial

Labels that keep clear.

Labels that never collide, and 3D projection, rays and picking that know when something is hidden. Rendered here by Three.js from the same scene.

explainerkit/zoom

Every scale, one camera.

From a street to the planet at a steady perceived speed, with levels of detail handing over as you go.

explainerkit/motion

Choreography, in a line.

Timelines, easing, springs and stagger. Order any number of parts from the first, the last or the centre.

explainerkit/inputs

Live input, live data.

Pointer, drag and drop, files, microphone, camera and device sensors arrive as ordinary parameters and data rows, validated and bounded. Bind them, and the scene reacts.

explainerkit/vfxexplainerkit/spatial

Into real footage.

Recover the camera from a recorded shot, then hang a chart on its wall and set things on its table. They keep their place as the camera moves. Tap the table to add one.

explainerkit/vfx

Measure what moves.

Read a velocity field from the footage itself, then draw it, trace it and let things ride it. Point at the water for its speed, or tap to launch a boat.
